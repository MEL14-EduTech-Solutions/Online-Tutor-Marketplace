# Problem Statement Document

## Problem Title
Online Tutor Marketplace (ETS Connect)

## Problem Description
Parents and students in Australia often face significant difficulty finding qualified academic tutors who are affordable, safe, and expert in the local curriculum. Many parents want to find one or several different tutors to help their children with various subjects, while students—ranging from K-12 to Higher Education—require targeted assistance to understand their schoolwork and successfully complete their assignments.

The problem is not just about finding any tutor; it is about establishing trust and ensuring academic quality in a virtual environment. Users must often navigate unverified listings without clear guidance on a tutor's background, their familiarity with the Australian curriculum, or a secure way to manage scheduling and payments. 

This lack of a centralized, vetted system can lead to academic frustration, financial risk due to payment disputes, and safety concerns regarding unverified individuals interacting with students.

## Target Users
The main users affected by this problem are:
- **Parents** looking for verified academic learning support for their children.
- **Students** from K-12 to Higher Education who need specific learning support to succeed.
- **Tutors** who want a faster, secure, and easier way to connect with students and manage their teaching business.

## Current Solutions
Current solutions include:
- **Online classifieds** such as Facebook Marketplace and Gumtree.
- **General online tutoring platforms** and global marketplaces.
- **University forums** and community messaging boards.
- **Word-of-mouth recommendations** from local school and community networks.

## Why Current Solutions Fail
Current solutions often fail parents and students for several reasons:
- **Lack of Verification:** There is often no proper identity or academic verification, particularly regarding mandatory Working with Children Checks (WWCC).
- **Insecure Logistics:** Manual booking and payment systems often lead to "no-shows," last-minute cancellations, or payment disputes.
- **Curriculum Mismatch:** Many platforms host global tutors who are unfamiliar with specific Australian curriculum standards (e.g., VCE, HSC, or the Australian National Curriculum).
- **Fragmented Experience:** Users are forced to use multiple tools for communication, scheduling, and payment, leading to a disorganized and high-effort experience.

## Proposed Solution
The proposed solution is a platform called **Online Tutor Marketplace (ETS Connect)**.

This platform provides a dedicated ecosystem for tutor discovery and management, offering a more secure and student-focused experience. It allows users to:
- **Discover Verified Tutors:** Search for tutors who have undergone mandatory identity, academic, and WWCC verification.
- **Secure Booking & Payment:** Utilize integrated scheduling and secure payment gateways to prevent disputes and coordination errors.
- **Access Virtual Learning Tools:** Use integrated live video, screen sharing, and digital whiteboards to facilitate effective remote learning.
- **Ensure Transparency:** Benefit from a transparent review and rating system to maintain high teaching standards.

## Australian Market Context
This problem is significant in Australia due to the high academic pressure in the K-12 and Higher Education sectors. Parents are increasingly looking for supplemental support but are wary of the safety risks associated with unverified informal tutors found on social media. 

In the Australian context, compliance with state-based child safety laws and alignment with local state curricula are essential. A solution that automates these checks and focuses on local curriculum expertise provides immense practical and safety value to the Australian community.

## Project Vision
The vision of this project is to make the process of finding and working with a tutor easier, safer, and more transparent for everyone involved. By bringing discovery, verification, and logistics into one secure place, the platform reduces uncertainty for parents and allows tutors to focus entirely on teaching.
